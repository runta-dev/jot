Preregistered before inference. Use R11.1's first-order, definitions-arm action/sense selections on its twelve previously seen prompts; these are development replays. Add four genuinely new advice prompts whose action and sense are chosen through the same generic verb tournament and WordNet enrichment before composition. No expected action, object, property or answer text is supplied.

For each selected full sense, retrieve a broad noun participant pool. Add noun lemmas present in the user request and the generic WordNet definition (provenance recorded), not a gold reply. Jev independently selects patient/result candidates, a primary source span and linker. Preserve multiple participants and generic valency alternatives rather than committing to intransitive early. Query an optional concrete property of the selected participant/result. Enumerate ordinary direct, prepositional, resultative and intransitive realizations, including realisable synonyms of the selected sense; compare whole sentences in bounded Choice groups.

Gate: >=9/12 development suggestions and >=3/4 fresh suggestions must be grammatical and offer a concrete plausible first step without merely restating the goal or inventing user facts. This tests advice only, not the full chat objective. Record cache provenance and newly incurred versus reused preparation separately. No live integration from this single experiment.
