After committing `Finally found`, the append-only loop cannot insert a missing subject before `found`. More text and delayed EOS cannot directly repair that committed error. The later ending follows from a malformed prefix. EOS remains permissive, but it is not the first causal failure.

## Decision

Do not add an EOS-only fix or larger vocabulary to address this failure. Correct grammatical continuation routes already existed. The relevant hypothesis is whether short lookahead or bounded revision can recover from greedy ranking mistakes while retaining general generation. Avoid hard-coded subject insertion, story templates, or manually supplied correct completions.

Next preregister a bounded lookahead experiment: at generic sentence-boundary continuation states, retain top few existing candidates, let the same Jev lexical mechanism extend each a few words, then compare longer rendered continuations. Candidate extensions must be produced by the same general algorithm, never evaluator-written. Include non-story contexts and added token/latency cost. Selection gains on fixed prefixes remain component evidence; a fresh full conversation is required before any integration. Repetition/stop limits still apply. Do not interpret score products as calibrated sequence probabilities.
