# R8.1 — reject bad complete answers, not just bad words

Preregistered before execution. Audit all ten literal R8 outputs, seven manually written positive controls, and one additional negative refraction control. Controls are validation data only; they must never become generation candidates or templates. No reference labels or sources are sent to Jev.

Ask four independent Nouls: material factual correctness, concrete responsiveness/usefulness, grammaticality, and correct conversational perspective. Read claims literally; do not silently substitute a more plausible actor or missing scientific qualifier. Accept only if all >=0.85 (fixed beforehand, not calibrated). Two runs with reversed case order.

Expected acceptance is fixed for clear cases. The sky metonymy and unqualified sunlight-to-energy shorthand are marked ambiguous and excluded from binary accuracy, while still fully reported. Clear errors include clothing itself evaporating, restating procrastination as advice, malformed because phrase, missing article, and changing the user's reading to the assistant's reading.

Scientific reference audit (researcher only, not model input): USGS evaporation page, NASA Wave Behaviors, DOE photosynthesis material. A failed validator is not made successful by changing thresholds after the run. No deployment from this small diagnostic.
