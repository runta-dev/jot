# R11 — same verbs, explicit senses

Preregistered before inference. Test whether supplying ordinary verb senses changes inappropriate lexical choices, without changing the candidate lemma set or providing an answer sentence.

Eight R10 development prompts reuse their exact final verb candidate sets. Four fresh prompts (meeting preparation, finding files, reducing online scrolling, difficult homework) obtain candidate sets via the same generic verb tournament used in R10; sets are then frozen in the manifest. Two deterministic orderings per case, with two conditions: bare lemma vs lemma plus first three WordNet verb senses in index order. Missing definitions are recorded, not fabricated or selectively replaced.

After each lemma choice, a separate Choice selects one of its supplied senses or NONE. This measures whether the intended use is licensed by a provided meaning; it does not repair the choice or rerun an incorrect answer. WordNet glosses are generic lexical data, not prompts written to solve individual tasks. Their provenance and license are preserved.

Record every chosen lemma, gloss, selected sense, and NONE outcome. Assess ordinary sense applicability and means-vs-outcome, not just keyword relevance. Advancement requires removing the documented card/vocabulary mismatch and plausible ordinary action senses on at least three of four fresh prompts; this only licenses a later sense-conditioned argument test. It does not prove a useful final recommendation or general chat.
