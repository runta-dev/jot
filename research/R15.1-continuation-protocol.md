# R15.1 rendered-continuation reranking

Preregistered before API execution. Independent implementation inspired by https://github.com/Code-Forge-AU/jev-llm revision 7ddd11876c2a84199baf84af43ee4a0274fbe764; no source copied. This tests the mechanism, not exact reproduction of that implementation or its performance claims.

Same first two conversations and real generated history as R15. Fixed 4096 words from the already checksummed Google common-word research corpus plus words in the conversation and declared Jev/TypeSafe identity. Research-only corpus, not a production dependency. No reference answers, manually chosen topical words or sentence templates. Deterministic capitalization/spacing only.

Per reply: round-robin 240-word groups, one Choice per group to select a word relevant to answering the conversation. Preserve first 400 common words, all conversation words, and top 32 words per group by within-group probabilities. No comparisons of probabilities across groups. Per next-word step, evaluate all active groups, preserve top four non-sentinel candidates per group; rerank all rendered full-prefix continuations in one Choice, with punctuation and EOS included. No speculative branches initially: measure quality before optimizing latency. Complete state includes all conversation messages and exact reply prefix. Maximum 40 word/punctuation steps, 180 seconds, 500,000 input tokens per reply; record budget truncation separately. End only on EOS or budget; no forced domain handlers.

Hypothesis: readable, contextually correct replies improve over 0/4 ASCII baseline. All four adequate replies required to advance to the remaining six conversations; any failure requires inspecting missing candidates versus ranking. Record full request/response traces, usage, exact output, finish reason, and latency. Human rubric from general-chat-v1.json. This small diagnostic cannot establish production suitability.
