No clean task pass under preregistered strict evaluation. 1–11 calls/case,301–7133ms; low latency on unsupported cases cannot be marketed as chat performance. A structurally valid statement can be unrelated to the conversational operation the user requested. The system behaves partly like a paraphraser, not an answer planner.

Decision: park this tree-first implementation. Keep parser/renderer/valency utilities as research artifacts, but do not spend further calls expanding a few task-specific constructions. It has repeatedly failed both old and fresh prompts. No production integration. Existing word-level decoder remains a research candidate with broader expressive evidence but insufficient reliability. A new trial must explain how its content selection differs materially from both tree-first paraphrasing and greedy continuation; protocol changes alone are not an implementation.
