# R15.4 operation conditioning results

Hypothesis not supported. Operation classification selects plausible labels, but adds no demonstrated improvement in follow-up adequacy. Both arms recall Mivora exactly and apply the green-bowl correction. Baseline simplification reduces its own longer answer to a short sentence; conditioned simplification repeats its already-short previous sentence verbatim. A correct classify step does not imply successful execution.

The baseline first explanation adds a claim about heat/molecular movement that is not a necessary explanation of evaporation; evaluate it as scientifically imprecise rather than silently treating all sentences as correct. The conditioned explanation is more concise, but this is one run and cannot be credited to the operation stage rather than inference variation. Different first replies mean the follow-up simplification burden differs; a controlled replay from a shared generated reply is needed for causal comparison.

Decision: do not add operation classification to the decoder. Retain full-continuation word selection as the simpler experimental baseline. Next evaluate the remaining unmeasured general-chat categories (advice with constraint, unknown/private fact, Chinese, creative continuation) as failure mapping, explicitly not advancement through the previously failed 4/4 gate. Stop tuning two easy categories. Any broad success still needs fresh held-out conversations, stability and cost validation. No production change.
