# R5.1 — compare realized sentences rather than independent slots

Preregistered after inspecting R5, before collecting this method's responses. R5 produced grammatical fragments with incorrect meanings: false treated as a predicate, provider/name mixed, and temporal details omitted. Its independent decisions did not reliably compose.

Keep R5's source-derived phrases, lemmatization and semantic-slot questions. Retain up to two options per slot with probability >=0.02, enumerate their product, rank by the product of returned probabilities, and realize at most the top 200 distinct core sentences. Exclude passive copular be (invalid grammar construction). Choose the best complete core text with one Jev Choice. This is deterministic candidate construction, not a supplied correct answer.

For each of at most two modifier steps, enumerate full realized sentences from every atomic source field value and a fixed generic relation set (from, by, in, on, at, with, for, to, of, because, bare temporal modifier, named apposition), plus unchanged. Choose between these whole texts, not independent relation/value fields. Contextual meaning and required-information coverage outrank fluent wording. All choices <=255; split if necessary.

Test the original eight as development plus four new frozen grounded facts: Nora bought tickets Tuesday; door locked=false; workers repair bridge daily; Omar open shop next week. No expected sentence is in the API request. Candidate sentences are combinatorially realized from facts and model probabilities.

Gate for further testing only: >=7/8 original and 4/4 fresh outputs must be grammatical, true, and cover explicitly requested information. No production switch. Report cost of core planning plus whole-candidate selection, and preserve failures. Source-grounded schema success does not establish open-domain knowledge, arbitrary discourse, recursion, or creative chat.
