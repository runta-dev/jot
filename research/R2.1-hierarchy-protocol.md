# R2.1 — bounded hierarchical word selection

Amendment preregistered after R2 transport rejection, before collecting any results of this method.

Observed API error for 4,096 options: HTTP 400, `{"detail":"Too many choices. Must have at most 255 choices."}`. R2 word attempts therefore provide no model-quality evidence. Character baselines remain valid and are saved in ../experiments/results/words-2026-09-18T02-23-46-459Z.

Keep R2's six prompts, lexicon, renderer, completion threshold, and reply-length budget unchanged. No expected answers or candidate sentences added.

Partition the deterministic 4,096-word-plus-context vocabulary into groups of at most 112 words. First Choice lists each group's members and selects a group for the next word; an independent Noul checks current reply completion. Take the two highest-probability ordinary groups (at most 224 words). A second Choice selects the actual next word from their union plus EOS (at most 225 options, below the observed 255 cap). If first-stage EOS is selected or Noul >= 0.9, terminate and record it. No future positions are generated in parallel.

Record both stages fully. This does not preserve perfect candidate recall: a good word in a discarded group becomes unreachable in the second stage. Candidate coverage and selection remain separate problems; no claim that grouping is lossless.

Budgets: at most 16 words, 120 seconds per response, two concurrent responses. Compare literal replies and actual measured costs against R2's character baseline. Pilot only; no automatic deployment.
