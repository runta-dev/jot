# R11.1 — full lexical context versus shortened gloss

Preregistered before inference. Hold lemma and candidate sense IDs fixed to the 24 definitions-arm selections in R11, plus its two card/vocabulary negative controls. Compare their cached short-gloss sense selections with a new selection that includes the same senses' synonyms, first example and applicable verb-frame patterns. No new sense or desired answer is added.

Purpose: shortened glosses such as break down can collapse distinct meanings (decomposition versus rotting). Full lexical context should prevent selecting a plainly unrelated sense without falsely treating a dictionary's limited coverage as proof that a contextual use is impossible.

Record changed synset IDs, NONE, examples and frames. This is a representation diagnostic on reused cases, not fresh generalization evidence. Lexical resources supply meaning descriptions; Jev selects them. No generated chat reply or deployment claim follows from a sense-selection change alone.
