# R15.9 bounded lookahead results

Source: ../experiments/results/lookahead-2026-09-18T07-47-25.446Z/. Three recorded development states, three automatically extended branches per state, three additional steps, two candidate orders. No reference completions inserted.

- Story: prefers `Finally, it ended.` over greedy `Finally found it.` in both orders. Grammar improves, but an ambiguous ending does not establish a useful narrative.
- Happier revision: prefers `Finally it was found!` over greedy `Finally found it!` in both orders. This corrects the observed missing-subject construction using generated branches, not manual repair. Still a minimal ending rather than a compelling revised story.
- Chinese explanation prefix: prefers `Because the earth turns away` over `Because earth turns away.` and tautological `Because it is night.` in both orders. The relationship to the Sun remains implicit.

All three comparisons choose a different branch from original greedy top candidate, stable in the two orderings. This is positive component evidence for seeing more context before committing. Not a complete conversation test or general stability estimate. Prefixes and active vocabulary originate from a previous run; candidate coverage still bounded.

Costs: 20 sequential research calls per state, 6.6–8.4 seconds, 66,241–74,974 input tokens. Appending this process at every word would be too expensive relative to the already expensive baseline. Parallelizing branches could reduce latency but not token cost, and has not been measured here.

Next: a preregistered full-conversation trial using bounded branching at sentence starts only, with a fixed per-reply branch budget. Preserve common generation path, full actual history and no gold seeds. Compare against ordinary morphological decoder on unseen prompts, assess coherence/compliance as well as grammar; track how often the branch budget is exhausted. No production integration justified yet.
